About the Game

Real Contract Rummy — on your phone

Kalooki is rooted in Jamaican rummy tradition and played worldwide—for fun and in competitive tournaments. Super Kalooki brings it to iOS and Android, for 4–6 players. Social, competitive, and no rule sheet required.

Nine scored deals. Contracts that change every round. The lowest cumulative score wins—go out fast, keep deadwood low, and watch your Jokers.
