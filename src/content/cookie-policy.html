What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to site owners. Cookies can be first-party (set by superkalooki.com) or third-party (set by external services we use).

Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary cookies — essential for the website to function. They cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with the site so we can improve it.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices you make to give you a better experience.

Consent Management — Cookiebot

We use Cookiebot, provided by Cybot A/S (Havnegade 39, 1058 Copenhagen, Denmark), to manage your cookie consent. When you first visit the site, Cookiebot presents a consent banner that lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. Your preferences are stored in a Cookiebot cookie so you are not asked again on future visits.

Cookiebot operates in auto-blocking mode on this site, meaning non-essential scripts are blocked from loading until you give consent. For more information see Cookiebot's privacy policy.

This site also implements Google Consent Mode v2. Before any consent signal is received, all Google data-storage categories (analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, functionality_storage, personalization_storage) are set to denied by default. Only security_storage is granted without consent, as it is necessary for basic site security. Ad-data redaction is enabled and URL click-ID passthrough is disabled, so no advertising identifiers are collected or forwarded to Google while consent is withheld. Once you accept the relevant cookie categories via the banner, Google services update their consent state accordingly.
