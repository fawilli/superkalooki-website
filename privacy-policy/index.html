Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the policies of Crofts Hill Holdings LLC on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use superkalooki.com or the Super Kalooki application. By using the Service you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Definitions

  • Company — Crofts Hill Holdings LLC, Florida, United States
  • Service — the superkalooki.com website and Super Kalooki application
  • Personal Data — information relating to an identified or identifiable individual
  • Usage Data — data collected automatically from the Service (e.g. pages visited, session duration)
  • You — the individual accessing or using the Service

Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of data:

  • Account data — email address, first and last name, phone number (where you provide them)
  • Usage data — IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, time and date of visit, time spent on pages, and unique device identifiers
  • Behavioural data — anonymised session recordings, heatmaps, and interaction patterns collected by analytics tools (see Third-Party Services below)

Third-Party Sign-In

You may sign in via Google, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or LinkedIn. If you do so, we may collect Personal Data associated with that account as permitted by the relevant platform's terms.

Application Permissions

With your prior permission the app may access your device location, contacts, and camera/photo library. You can change these permissions at any time through your device settings.

Third-Party Analytics & Tracking Services

We use the following third-party services to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it. Each service only activates after you give consent via our cookie banner (powered by Cookiebot). You can withdraw consent at any time through the cookie settings link in the footer.

Cookiebot (Consent Management)

Provided by Cybot A/S (Havnegade 39, 1058 Copenhagen, Denmark). Cookiebot manages your cookie consent preferences and stores them so you are not asked again on future visits. It operates in auto-blocking mode, preventing non-essential scripts from loading until you consent. See the Cookiebot privacy policy.

Google Analytics 4

Provided by Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA). Google Analytics collects anonymised data about pages visited, session duration, and general geographic region to help us improve site content and performance (property ID: G-SL2JT4PC9T). Data may be transferred to and stored in the United States. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can opt out via the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Microsoft Clarity

Provided by Microsoft Corporation (One Microsoft Way, Redmond, WA 98052, USA). Microsoft Clarity records anonymised session replays and generates heatmaps showing how visitors interact with the site (project ID: wmxjtxj1w5). Clarity does not collect personally identifiable information. Data may be processed in the United States. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement and the Clarity privacy disclosure.

For full details of the cookies set by each of these services, see our Cookie Policy.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • To manage your account and authenticate you
  • To contact you with service-related updates and, where you have opted in, marketing communications
  • To analyse usage and improve the site and app
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell your Personal Data. We may share data with third-party service providers (such as those listed above) solely to operate the Service, and only to the extent necessary. We may disclose data where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of the Company or others.

Retention & Security

We retain Personal Data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use commercially reasonable security measures to protect your data, though no internet transmission is 100% secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your Personal Data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@superkalooki.com.

Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed at anyone under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
